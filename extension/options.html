Upvance Assistant – Options

Where your Upvance app runs. Default http://localhost:3100 (matches PORT in .env).

Create one in Upvance → Settings → Chrome extension pairing. It's stored only in this browser.

What this extension does

  • Only acts when you click a button in its popup.
  • Reads the job or profile you are viewing and sends it to your local app.
  • Fills employer forms from the package you approved – never clicks Submit.
  • Never logs in to sites, never sends messages, doesn't support LinkedIn Easy Apply.
